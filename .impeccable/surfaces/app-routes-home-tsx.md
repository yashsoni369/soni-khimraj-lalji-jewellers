## Scope
Whole-site redesign (Persuade mode).
- **Existing 6 routes, same URLs:** `/`, `/gold-rate-today-ghatkopar`, `/bridal-jewellery`, `/old-gold-exchange`, `/silver-jewellery`, `/gold-dream-scheme`.
- **3 new pages:** Our Story, Collections lookbook, Gemstones (noindex until confirmed).
- **Shared:** header, footer, sticky phone bar, booking dialog, 404.

## Audience and job
Local families, old-gold sellers and scheme savers in and around Ghatkopar, mostly on a phone. They check the rate, look at the pieces, then WhatsApp Mr. Soni, call, get directions or book a visit.

## Proof
- The real shop-front photo.
- The live IBJA rate.
- BIS 916 / HUID.
- Testing and weighing in front of the customer.
- 25+ years on Jivdaya Lane.
- No invented reviews, prices or pieces.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The site is the jeweller's counter you sit at. Pieces lie on bottle-green velvet trays, gold is tested on the touchstone and weighed on the scale in front of you, and the day's rate sits under glass. It refuses the maroon-gold-serif jeweller template and the cart-led e-commerce shell.

OWN-WORLD:
- **Palette:** near-black counter, bottle-green velvet, brass-gold type and rules, teak, and glass white.
- **Gold:** only in the photographs and in brass type and rules; no gold gradients or glow.
- **Components:** velvet tray cards with brass name tags, brass-framed glass panels for rates and facts, and a touchstone streak as the old-gold mark.
- **Type:** the Devanagari shop-sign name, a refined serif for display, and a clean sans for body.

STORY: Visitors see a real counter, believe they'll be treated honestly (tested and weighed in front of them, the rate shown openly), and act by WhatsApping Mr. Soni, calling, getting directions or booking a visit.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- **Background:** a full-bleed overhead counter photo with the velvet tray (kundan necklace, bangles), touchstone streak, scale and loupe.
- **Left third, on the velvet:**
  - the Devanagari name;
  - the two-line serif headline "Tested in front of you. / Weighed in front of you.";
  - the line "BIS 916 hallmarked gold · 25+ years on Jivdaya Lane";
  - a white "WhatsApp Mr. Soni" button and a brass "Bring your old gold →" link.
- **Bottom right:** a glass rate label with the live IBJA 22K rate and date.
- **Header:** a slim near-black bar with the nav, the phone number and a WhatsApp icon.

FORM: The Velvet Counter, the 1st-ranked grounded candidate in re-roll round 1 (IMPECCABLE'S PICK), seed ea10919a, composite comp approved.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
